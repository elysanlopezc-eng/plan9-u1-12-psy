Stage II: Dual-Perspective Consumer Explanation (~15 Minutes)

Explain a real-world purchasing choice by integrating TWO distinct psychological perspectives.

Real-World Application Challenge

Scenario: "The Sudden Upgrade to an Eco-Friendly Electric Car"

A consumer trades in a perfectly functional gas-powered sedan to buy a high-end electric vehicle. Explain this behavior using TWO lenses:

Perspective 1: Humanistic (Self-Actualization)

The buyer seeks alignment with their core personal values regarding environmental stewardship, deriving a sense of personal growth and authenticity.

Perspective 2: Sociocultural (Social Proof & Status)

The buyer conforms to the expectations of their peer group, signaling environmental awareness and modern social status within their community.

Student Pair Activity:

Select any consumer product (e.g., luxury coffee, gaming console) and write down a 2-sentence explanation combining Cognitive + Behavioral or Biological + Evolutionary perspectives.

Stage IV: Reflection on Psychology & Ethical Marketing (~10 Minutes)

Class reflection on ethical responsibilities when applying psychological tools to consumer behavior.

The Ethical Marketer's Creed

  • Respect consumer autonomy and psychological vulnerability.
  • Use evidence-based insights to inform and add genuine value.
  • Reject deceptive dark patterns, fake scarcity, and false testimonials.

Montano Habit: Think Win-Win

True commercial success comes from creating Win-Win transactions where the consumer receives real satisfaction and the brand builds long-term trust, guided by scientific understanding.
